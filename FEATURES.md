# Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy — Features

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy tools for PC with chapter profiles, damage controls, combat pacing and encounter practice. Revisit Sophia's journey with a configuration tailored to exploration or combat.

## Chapter profiles

Save progress profiles organized by chapter.

## Damage controls

Adjust incoming damage for your preferred combat difficulty.

## Combat pacing

Change practice speed to work on encounter timing.

## Encounter retries

Return to a saved encounter state and try another approach.

## Exploration notes

Keep puzzle and collection notes alongside chapter progress.

## Assistance presets

Switch between saved exploration and combat configurations.

## Profiles

Select a chapter profile, adjust damage and pacing, then practise the encounter. Reduce assistance on later attempts and keep your preferred settings.
